🛰 Real event · May 2024

The “Gannon storm”

  • The strongest geomagnetic storm in over 20 years: G5 (extreme) on 10–11 May, the first G5 since the Halloween storms of October 2003.
  • Source: sunspot region AR 13664, with 12 X-class flares between 8 and 15 May and several Earth-directed CMEs.
  • Auroras were seen across Europe, much further south than usual.
  • Named in memory of space weather physicist Dr Jennifer Gannon, who died on 2 May 2024.
  • For astronauts the danger is the proton storm.
Sources: NASA DONKI · NOAA SWPC · NASA CCMC · Hayakawa et al., ApJ 2025 · DONKI event ↗
