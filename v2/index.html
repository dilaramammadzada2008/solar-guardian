Design basis

Solar Guardian is a prototype of the crew-facing self-care front end of an onboard clinical decision support system (CDSS), as described by NASA's Human Research Program, Exploration Medical Capability (ExMC):
· NASA ExMC, Clinical Decision Support System Concept of Operations, HRP-48033 (2022). NTRS 20220017856: crew self-care, symptom logging, follow-up status (resolved / improved / no change / worsened), cabin atmosphere identification across crew.
· Parker, Krihak et al., Earth-Independent Medical Operations (EIMO) Concept of Operations, HRP IWS (2025). NTRS 20250000009: gradual transfer of medical decisions onboard; "self-medical management and behavioral health" scenario.
· EIMO Datascope Technical Interchange Meeting (2023). NTRS 20240001278: a "system of systems" combining wearables, environment, countermeasures, behavioral data and point-of-care tests.
· NASA OCHMO, Integrated Medical Model (IMM) overview: a probabilistic model of 100 spaceflight medical conditions in three categories (environmental, injury/trauma, medical illness). Solar Guardian classifies each health item with these categories. nasa.gov
· Google Public Sector (8 Aug 2025), How Google and NASA are testing AI for medical care in space: the Crew Medical Officer Digital Assistant (CMO-DA), a proof-of-concept CDSS that supports the CMO. Solar Guardian is not CMO-DA; it collects the crew member's data and context in a structured form such a tool could use. cloud.google.com
Simulated data for demonstration. Not a medical device. Decision rules are simple, transparent and listed with each outcome; clinical thresholds would be set by flight surgeons.
