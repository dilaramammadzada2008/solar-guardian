SOLAR GUARDIAN · Contactless pulse test

Remote photoplethysmography (rPPG): heart rate from tiny colour changes in the skin of your face. Video never leaves this device. Not a medical device.
How to measure
  1. Put your face inside the oval. Light should fall on your face from the front (a window or lamp behind the laptop).
  2. Sit still, don't talk or smile, breathe normally.
  3. Wait 15–20 seconds.
