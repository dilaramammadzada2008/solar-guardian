How to measure
  1. Cover the camera lens fully with your fingertip. Press lightly, not hard.
  2. Laptop: shine a phone flashlight through your finger from behind (nail side).
  3. Phone: use the back camera; the flashlight turns on automatically.
  4. Sit still for 15–20 seconds.
